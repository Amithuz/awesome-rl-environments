A curated list of companies, platforms, and open-source projects building reinforcement learning (RL) environments for training and evaluating AI agents.

RL environments are the simulated, resettable settings — a codebase to debug, a browser to navigate, a synthetic enterprise app to operate — where agents attempt real tasks and receive reward signals during reinforcement learning. As gains from pre-training flatten, environments (and the human feedback that grades them) have become one of the most important inputs in frontier AI. This list tracks who's building them. Maintained by the team at Huzzle Labs as part of RL Atlas, a ranked directory of the space. We build in this field and are listed here alongside everyone else. Contents

Open Source & Tooling

Prime Intellect — Open superintelligence stack with a public Environments Hub, the PRIME-RL framework, verifiers, and open RL model releases. HUD — Wraps arbitrary software (browsers, games, apps) into dockerized, MCP-exposed environments; infrastructure for building your own. General Reasoning — Environments and reasoning data for long-horizon, multi-agent reliability, with open community releases. From ex-Meta FAIR researchers. Bespoke Labs — Open datasets and reproducible post-training and evaluation recipes.

Code & Software Engineering

Mechanize — A small set of high-fidelity software-engineering environments and graders for frontier coding agents. Datacurve — Code-execution and evaluation environments plus coding data for tight training loops. AfterQuery — Benchmark environments for code and finance workflows with real-task framing.

Browser & Computer Use

Halluminate — Resettable computer-use sandboxes (e.g., a synthetic CRM) and datasets; strong financial-services focus. Plato — Browser-interaction environments fused with enterprise workflow simulation. Matrices — Browser-native environments for web navigation and interaction tasks.

Long-Horizon & Reasoning

General Reasoning — Long-horizon, multi-agent environments and reasoning data. Huzzle Labs — Long-horizon coding and agent environments paired with vetted human-expert feedback.

Finance & Domain-Specific

Halluminate — Computer-use environments with a financial-services focus. AfterQuery — Code and finance benchmark environments. Mercor — Domain-specific environments (code, healthcare, law) sourced through an expert marketplace.

Human-Data Platforms Companies that grew from human-data and labeling work and now build environments too.

Surge AI — Bootstrapped human-data leader now building RL environments in-house. Scale AI — Data-labeling incumbent extending into agents and environments via Forge. Mercor — Expert marketplace offering domain-specific environments at frontier scale. Huzzle Labs — RLHF datasets and evaluation benchmarks alongside engineered environments.
